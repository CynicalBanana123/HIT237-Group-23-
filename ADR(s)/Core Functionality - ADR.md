# Core Functionality

## 31/03/2026 Revision:

### Status:
Approved

### Context
Application must have a core functionality to address the problem scenario of; Tenants in remote community's log housing repair requests (broken doors, no AC, plumbing), track status, and view maintenance history for their dwelling.

### Alternative Solutions Considered
1.	Forum
  - Pros
    - Easily accessible
    - Easy to create and modify
  - Cons
    - Risk of public postings being abused 
    - Misattributions
    - Difficult for services to track and stay up to date with
2.	Contact Pages for Contractor Information
  - Pros
    - Provides tenants control over when and what contractors can be brought on to assist them with property upkeep.
    - Tenant's responsibility to provide information to the contacted contractor
  - Cons
    - Difficult to track maintenance history without additional or external means of reporting by either the contractor or tenant.

### Solution Decided Upon:
Ticket System – Users can submit tickets to request repairs for their homes and properties. These tickets can also be logged for later referrals, if needed. These types of systems are numerous, providing both regular users and administrators with familiarity they can draw on when navigating the application.

### Consequences:
Means by which to properly address tickets to the correct property must be organised to prevent accidental or deliberate misattribution. As well as measures to prevent users from breaching others' privacy by accessing other user-submitted tickets.
